# Field survey notes

The survey team visited eleven sites along the northern ridge during the spring season. Each site was photographed, measured and described in a short report written on the same day.

## 1 Method

Measurements were taken with a calibrated tape and a handheld level. Where the ground was uneven, the team repeated every reading twice and kept the average.

1. Mark the corners of the plot
2. Record the slope and the soil type
3. Photograph the plot from the south

## 2 Findings

Most plots showed moderate erosion on the western side. Two sites near the river had been flooded recently and were excluded from the final count.
