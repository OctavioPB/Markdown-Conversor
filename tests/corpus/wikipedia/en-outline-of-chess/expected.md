# Outline of chess

The following [outline](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_(list)>) is provided as an overview of and topical guide to [chess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess):

Chess is a [two-player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-player_game) [strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy_game) [board game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_game) [played](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play_(activity)>) on a [chessboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chessboard) with 32 [pieces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_piece).

## Descriptions of chess

Chess can be described as all of the following:

- Form of [entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment) – form of activity that holds the attention and interest of an audience, or gives pleasure and delight.
  - Form of [recreation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recreation) – activity of leisure, leisure being discretionary time.[^1]
    - Form of [play](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play_(activity)>) – voluntary, intrinsically motivated activity normally associated with recreational pleasure and enjoyment.[^2]
      - [Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game) – structured playing, usually undertaken for enjoyment and sometimes used as an educational tool.
        - [Board game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_game) – game in which counters or pieces are placed, removed, or moved on a pre-marked surface or "board" according to a set of rules.
        - [Strategy game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy_game) – Game in which the players' decision-making skills have a high significance in determining the outcome.
        - [Two-player game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-player_game) – game played by just two players, usually against each other.
      - Sport – form of play, but sport is also a category of entertainment in its own right *(see immediately below for description)*
  - [Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport) – Competitive, entertaining, and skillful activity. It has rules and a winner can be decided by objective means.[^3]
    - [Mind sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_skill) – game where the outcome is determined mainly by mental skill, rather than by pure chance.

## Chess equipment

Main article: [Chess equipment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_equipment)

### Essential equipment

*Opening chess position from black side*

- [Chess set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_set) – Equipment required to play a game of chess, they come in various materials and styles.
  - [Chessboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chessboard) – Board with 64 squares arranged in two alternating colors.
    - [Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_chess#rank) – horizontal row of squares on the chessboard.
    - [File](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_chess#file) – vertical (i.e. in the direction from one player to the other) column of squares on the chessboard.
  - 32 [chess pieces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_piece) in two colors, referred to as "white" and "black".
    - 2 [kings](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_(chess)>)
    - 2 [queens](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_(chess)>) – Some chess sets have 2 queens of each color, to deal with [promotions](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_(chess)>).
    - 4 [rooks](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rook_(chess)>)
    - 4 [bishops](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_(chess)>)
    - 4 [knights](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_(chess)>)
    - 16 [pawns](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawn_(chess)>)

### Specialized equipment

*[Chess clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_clock) on a [chess table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_table)*

- [Chess clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_clock) – Dual timer used to monitor each player's thinking time. Used for speed chess, and to regulate time in tournament games.
- [Chess table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess_table) – Table built with features to make it useful for playing chess, most are made of wood.
- [Score sheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_chess#score_sheet) and writing implement – Tournament games require scores to be kept, and many players like to record other games for later analysis.
